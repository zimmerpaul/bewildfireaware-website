PSICC High Plains FDRA — ERC & Largest Fires on Record

What it shows

Only one fire here supports day-by-day ERC comparison. Bridger (2008) is the single fire in this FDRA with a usable daily growth timeline paired with station ERC, so no pooled statistics are shown. Its 6 significant growth days averaged the 92nd percentile of the FDRA's Mar–Oct ERC record, versus the 94th on its other active days.

The biggest runs cluster on high-ERC days. Bridger's biggest run (+32,000 ac on Jun 12, 2008) came at the 91st percentile of ERC.

Within-fire, day-to-day correlation varies (Bridger ρ = -0.34). ERC measures fuel dryness, not wind — the day-to-day trigger for the largest runs is often wind on top of dry fuels, which is worth remembering because wind is not part of the ERC calculation. This is a small, size-selected sample — the 3 largest fires on record in this FDRA (the pocket card for this FDRA charts no individual fires) — so these numbers describe these incidents, not a general model for PSICC High Plains FDRA.

Method & caveats

Fire growth. Cumulative acreage per ICS-209 incident situation report (ICS-209-PLUS v2.0 research dataset, 1999–2020). Where reports skip days, the acreage gain is spread evenly across the gap. Final acreages shown match the fire-matching manifest: Badger Hole 50,671; Bridger 45,800; MM 117 42,795.

ERC. FEMS daily NFDR summaries (fuel model X — Brush) for the FDRA's stations — Pinion Canyon (56202), Ute Canyon (56301), Cimarron (148601) — downloaded from the FEMS climatology API. SIG daily ERC is the mean of stations reporting that day. Unlike the earlier UCR Middle Elevation report, which had to substitute gridMET ERC-G, this report uses station ERC on the FDRA's own fuel-model scale, so thresholds here are the FDRA's own percentiles: Mar–Oct 2005–2024 90th percentile = 92.3, 97th = 97.4 (4,900 station-mean days). FEMS returns no NFDR records for these stations before 2005-01-01 (1999–2004 queried, empty), so pre-2005 fires appear without ERC.

Statistics. “Significant growth day” = daily gain ≥2% of final fire size (min 100 ac). ρ is Spearman rank correlation between daily ERC and daily growth over each fire's active days; group differences (significant vs. other days) use a Mann–Whitney U normal approximation. Fires with fewer than 4 situation reports, stub timelines, or no station ERC for their year are shown for context and excluded from pooled statistics. This is a small, non-random sample of memorable fires — results describe these incidents, not a general model.

Sources: ICS-209-PLUS v2.0 (figshare 19858927; Nature Sci. Data 2023) · FEMS (fems.fs2c.usda.gov) NFDR daily summaries · NWCG pocket cards · WFIGS and contemporary news coverage where noted. Generated July 2026 for bewildfireaware.com.
